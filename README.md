MSPGA - Make Stock Parts Great Again
A Stock and Stockalike Parts Rebalancer for RSS

MSPGA is a lightweight rebalance mod designed to bridge the gap between stock Kerbal Space Program gameplay and the extreme realism of full Realism Overhaul.

Stock KSP parts were never designed for Real Solar System scale. Tanks are excessively heavy, engine performance is artificially limited, and stock vehicles quickly become impractical once orbital velocities and real planetary scales enter the equation.

MSPGA addresses this by systematically rebalancing stock and stockalike parts for RSS-scale gameplay while preserving the familiar construction style, simplicity, and accessibility of stock KSP.

This is not Realism Overhaul.

This is not RP-1.

This is a cleaner, lighter “RO-Lite” experience focused on making realistic-scale missions achievable using familiar parts and stock-style gameplay.

Features
Rebalanced stock and stockalike engine performance
Improved dry mass fractions for fuel tanks
RSS-oriented thrust and ISP tuning
Support for legacy and modern _v2 stock parts
TweakScale compatibility tuning
Stock-friendly progression and construction
Modular ModuleManager-based architecture
Lightweight and highly compatible
Design Philosophy

MSPGA aims for believable aerospace scaling rather than full simulation complexity.

The goal is not perfect real-world replication. The goal is to make rockets feel appropriate for realistic planetary scales while retaining the creativity and accessibility that make KSP enjoyable.

Expect:

More practical launch vehicles
Improved payload fractions
Realistic-ish staging behavior
More meaningful upper stages
Better large-scale vehicle scaling

without:

procedural avionics
mandatory realism mechanics
ignition management
ullage simulation
career grind overhead
